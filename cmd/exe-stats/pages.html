Stats

See who reads your websiteSimple, private visitor stats from one line of code.

Stats counts the people who open your pages and shows you, hour by hour, how many came, what they read, which links and searches sent them, and which countries and devices they came from.

Setup
Add your site, then paste the one line of code it gives you into your pages. Numbers show up as soon as people visit.
Privacy
No cookies, nothing saved in visitors' browsers, and no IP addresses stored. Visitors are told apart by an anonymous code that changes every midnight. The code is open source.
Access
For holders of {{.Token}}, a token on Solana. Sign in with your wallet: the more it holds, the more sites you can add and the more views a month are counted. Nothing is charged, and signing in never sends a transaction.
