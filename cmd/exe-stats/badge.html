Link to StatsAn 88×31 button for your page.

Before there were dashboards, a page counted its own readers: a row of number wheels at its foot, beside a row of buttons like this one, each a link to something its author made or liked. This button is a counter too. Its wheels count a day's readers while the day's bars fill, midnight turns them back to nought, and a click brings a reader to Stats.

A site counted here can have one of its own, with its own numbers on the wheels: choose a style in the site's settings.

Put it in a footer, a sidebar or a README. Paste one of these as it is, or save the GIF and serve it yourself.
